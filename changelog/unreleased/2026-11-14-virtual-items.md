- `El::virtual_items(Rc<dyn VirtualItems>)` mounts application-selected, absolutely positioned keyed windows without re-running the view when only the window changes. Staying subtrees retain their bindings, memos and focus; entering items mount settled within a per-frame priority budget, leaving items retire immediately, and focused items remain mounted until focus leaves. Indexed virtual lists can share the tree, and partial window commits now refresh live glyphs while preserving their authored placeholder measurements.
